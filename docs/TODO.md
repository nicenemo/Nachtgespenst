Source `site/` on `develop`, published to `gh-pages` by `.github/workflows/pages.yml` on every push that touches `site/` ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-project-site-on-gh-pages.md)). `site/index.html` came from a throw-away generator that is not kept: edit the HTML directly, refresh screenshots with `SerialTest --shot`.
- The Core2 BLE bond lives on the USB dongle (hci1), the CoreS3 on hci0. After an S3 flash the evdev numbers for USB and BLE are often swapped; `SerialTest` matches by `Phys`. The evdev counter must always be reading while keys are sent (the kernel buffer of about 64 events overflows otherwise).
- `KEY` and `TAP` on a keyboard page really type on the host; test on the Locks page with Scroll Lock.
- Everything about running commands, flashing and testing: [claude-code-workflow](wiki/claude-code-workflow.md), [hardware-testing](wiki/hardware-testing.md), [troubleshooting](wiki/troubleshooting.md).
