# Microphone and oscilloscope
- The board records 512 samples at 16 kHz and sends the waveform
- The PC finds a trigger point, scales it and draws a scope
- The picture goes back as lamps: an 80 x 47 grid, about 13 frames per second
- The PC plays 440, 880 and 1500 Hz as a test signal
