# The hardware
- CoreS3: ESP32-S3, USB and Bluetooth LE, SD card, 320 x 240 touchscreen
- Core2: ESP32, Bluetooth LE only, three touch buttons under the screen
- Both: LED bar of 10 WS2812 lamps (M5GO base), microphone, speaker
- No extra parts: everything below runs on these two boards
